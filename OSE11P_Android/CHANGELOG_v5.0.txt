MONITORTUNE v5.0 — DELCO CALIBRATION WORKBENCH

Adds TunerPro XDF XML metadata and ADX XML metadata inspection, table/constant catalogue, conservative unsigned contiguous BIN preview with manually supplied offset, AFR-based VE correction advisor capped at +/-20%, and unit tests.

Important: this is not full TunerPro compatibility. Complex address bases, bank switching, bit offsets, signed values, endian flags, strides, axes, equations, packet framing, checksums and ECU-specific requests require additional implementation. The importer does not automatically decode all ADX streams or apply XDF math to table values.

No live ECU calibration write or flash operation is included. VE advice is review-only. Hardware and full Android validation remain outstanding.
