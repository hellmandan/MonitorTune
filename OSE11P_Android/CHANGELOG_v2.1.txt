MonitorTune 2.1 - development upgrade

ADDED
- ISO-TP single-frame and multi-frame response reassembly utility for diagnostic payloads.
- VIN decoder now uses ISO-TP reassembly and validates a 17-character VIN.
- Expanded generic DTC description catalogue with safe manufacturer-specific fallbacks.
- Persistent vehicle profiles: make/model/year, engine, ECU, protocol, fuel, mass, notes and modifications.
- CSV session logger with timestamps and generic OBD live-data channels.
- Generic sensor plausibility warnings to flag impossible/out-of-range readings without claiming a fault.

RETAINED
- Existing OSE $11P V104 / USB ALDL source and tuning functionality.
- Existing Bluetooth Classic ELM327 live data, DTC polling, dyno and AI diagnostic components.

LIMITATIONS / SAFETY
- ISO-TP reassembly handles response frames emitted by the adapter. It does not implement arbitrary raw-CAN transmission, custom flow-control timing, J1939 transport protocol, or manufacturer-specific diagnostic sessions.
- DTC text is generic. Correct repair guidance requires make/model/year/ECU-specific definitions and service data.
- SessionLogger and VehicleProfileStore are reusable components; they are not yet wired into visible screens or automatic recording controls in this source snapshot.
- Sensor plausibility limits are broad generic checks, not calibration limits for every vehicle.
- Road-dyno estimates are not a substitute for a calibrated chassis dyno. Mass, gradient, wind, drivetrain loss, tyre size, gear ratio and sensor accuracy materially affect results. Do not perform testing on public roads.
- No ECU write, flash, coding, actuator test or DTC-clear operation is enabled by these additions.
